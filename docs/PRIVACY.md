# Privacy Policy for Shifty

_Last updated: June 13, 2026_

Shifty does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

## What Shifty stores

Everything you enter — shifts, jobs, hourly rates, tips, mileage, expenses, notes, workplace locations, and your settings — is stored **on your device**.

If you enable iCloud Sync, that same data is also stored in **your own private iCloud database**, so it can sync between your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. Apple provides that storage; the developer of Shifty has no access to it, and the data is not visible to anyone else. You can turn iCloud Sync off in Settings at any time.

## Permissions Shifty may ask for

- **Notifications** — only to remind you about upcoming shifts, prompt you to log tips when a shift ends, and deliver an optional weekly summary. Notifications are scheduled on your device.
- **Calendar (write only)** — only when you choose to export shifts as calendar events. Shifty cannot read your existing calendar events.

Shifty does **not** request access to your location. Workplace locations are places you search for and select yourself; your device's position is never requested or recorded.

## Apple Intelligence features

On supported devices, Shifty can read a shift description or a pasted schedule and turn it into shift entries. This runs entirely **on your device** using Apple's on-device model. The text is not uploaded anywhere and is not stored beyond what you choose to save.

## Data you export

CSV and PDF exports are created at your request and saved wherever you choose. Shifty does not send them anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Data → Delete All Data removes every shift and job from the device and, if iCloud Sync is on, from your iCloud database. Deleting the app also removes its local data.

## Children

Shifty is a general-purpose work tool and is not directed at children.
